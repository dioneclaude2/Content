01 — Findings

It's not about being famous. It's a few simple tricks.

We checked every step against the brand's own Instagram posts, not news articles. Brands with no celebrity still got tens of thousands of likes.

Honest caveat: only one brand ran a public giveaway and then a limited drop with every step on Instagram: Dr. Squatch × Sydney Sweeney. Others either dropped first and then gave away (e.l.f. × Liquid Death) or gave free PR boxes before the drop (Gentle Monster).

03 — The Plan

The Monsters moved out. Only Monster Lovers get in.

Which monster are you?

Lemmy, Jack and Medusa got too naughty for Nancy's house, so they moved into their own Mansion: the new monster account. Following it means you're one of the Residents. The question that pulls everyone in: “Which monster are you?”

Tone: naughty and iconic, never spooky. No Halloween clichés (no cobwebs or cheesy ghosts).
