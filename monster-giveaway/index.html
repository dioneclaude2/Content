It's not about being famous. It's a few simple tricks.

5.6Ads. Small and safe.

Boost only the giveaway post22–27 Oct. Ad goal: profile visits to the monster account (not likes).
Never the product's useShow the Mansion + characters. Meta doesn't allow ads that sell or show adult toys. Sexual wellness ads are only allowed when they focus on health, not pleasure.
Audience 18+Existing followers, site visitors and customers first, then people similar to them. A broad giveaway audience brings in “freebie hunters”.
No ads for the drop itselfHave someone check the creative against Meta's adult-products rules before spending. The rules change.
Free reach matters more than ads: co-host the giveaway with a bigger partner brand (the Crocs × Juicy trick), have creators unbox the PR Mansions and tag both accounts, and use Instagram's Collab post so one post shows on every account.

5.7Rules + next steps.

Giveaway rules must include: 18+, US only (or the eligible countries), start/end date and time, how the winner is picked and contacted, no purchase necessary, and “not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Instagram”. Get them checked before launch (not legal advice).
