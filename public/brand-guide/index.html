02

The work

What we need back

Finished content, not a set of cuts.

An edit you would post yourself. Every choice made on purpose, and traceable to something that already worked.

What you will receive

Footage lands day by day in the shared folder, not all at once. Start on a day as soon as its folder is up.

Two passes of everything

A clean digital pass and a lo-fi one. Use both in the same cut and do not grade them to match — the difference is the look.

Talking footage

Street interviews and panels. Caption it, censor what needs it before export, and keep only the clips that actually say something.

Stills

Your source for photo reels. Cut them fast and to the beat, exactly like a highlights edit.

Location sound

Real audio from the day. Keep it under the music — it is what stops a cut sounding like stock.

Work it in this order: watch the day through once, pull your selects, find the track, then cut. The blurry and lo-fi frames are selects, not rejects.

What to make

Carousels and founder picks are handled in-house. We have an editor assigned to both, so leave them. They appear at the bottom for context only.

Rules

Do
  • Find the reference first. Build the idea out of it.
  • Find the track before you cut.
  • Cut on movement. Match the reference’s pulse.
  • Keep location sound under the music.
  • Keep the lo-fi and blurry frames. They are selects.
Don’t
  • Cut off the beat. If the edit is not riding the track, it is not finished.
  • Edit first and look for the reference afterwards.
  • Decorate the type. No strokes, shadows, plates or gradients. Cream white or Tickled Pink, nothing else.
  • Colour-match the clean and lo-fi passes together.
  • Let a fourth element onto the frame.
