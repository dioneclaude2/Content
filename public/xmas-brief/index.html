He's quiet, shower-safe, and charges on a magnetic cable.

Jack
Suction + Vibration

Arrives thirsty, leaves hungry for more. Small, but he gets around. Neon only this edition, and the hardest one to find.

"Don't let the smile fool you."

Jack is a palm-sized pumpkin with two motors. Turn him over and there's a soft opening in the base: that's the suction, gentle air pulses that start soft and build over 11 levels. A second motor makes the whole pumpkin vibrate, with 7 speeds and 3 patterns. Use one, or press both buttons for a bigger finish. He's fully waterproof and charges by USB-C.
